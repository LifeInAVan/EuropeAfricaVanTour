# VW LT31 Westfalia Florida — History, Modifications and Maintenance

Oct 26, 2026 · @Peter

## The van

A 1993-built VW LT31 with a Westfalia Florida camper conversion, first registered in Germany in March 1994 and in my hands since September 2011. It has covered about 140,000 miles, roughly 35,000 of them with me.

- **Engine:** ACL 2.4 litre six-cylinder turbodiesel, 95 hp (70 kW), Garrett turbo. Same D24 family Volvo used in the 240/740 and Steyr-Puch in the Pinzgauer.
- **Body:** Westfalia Florida high-top camper, single rear wheels.
- **Germany, 1994–2008:** three German owners. The longest, in Kiel, kept it about 12 years and had the engine rebuilt in 1999 (see below). It was driven as far as Italy around 2003.
- **UK, 2008–2011:** imported to the UK, then a keeper in Aberystwyth from 2009. Sold through Campervans4U, a Westfalia specialist in Essex.
- **Since 20 Sep 2011:** based in Edinburgh. Most work is done by me; workshops handle MOTs, servicing, gearbox-out and specialist jobs.
- **The big trip:** Edinburgh to Pärnu, Estonia and back via Latvia and Germany, Aug–Sep 2023 — 2,454 miles in 25 days at 20.9 mpg ([video: Camper to Estonia](https://youtu.be/cr-nd-LwLs0)).

## Mechanical modifications

Running gear, wheels and turbo work. The engine internals and injection pump are standard.

- **Suspension lift (Aug 2020):** 3 in (75 mm) LT Mk1 lift kit with heavy-duty front coil springs and new rear dampers, fitted by Wheeliams in Scarborough.
- **Wheels and tyres (Aug 2020):** 15 in Ford Transit wheels in place of the stock 14 in, on new tyres, with conversion studs and open nuts replacing the wheel bolts.
- **Spare wheel (Oct 2023):** moved to a rear-mounted carrier.
- **Towbar and rear step (2026):** the heavy steel-beam VW factory towbar, fitted while the diesel tank was out, bolted into threaded sockets in both chassis rails, with Hella 13-pin electrics. A removable steel step clamps round the towbar, epoxy-coated with an aluminium tread plate. [Shed7 — Towbar and step](https://reports.workphotos.com/6ad5b30ce0d4dbcb7715ce8b).
- **Roof rack:** Wheeliams BOATRAK.
- **Exhaust:** stainless system, wrapped, with a T3 turbo blanket.
- **Instruments:** Stack boost gauge, oil temperature gauge, and exhaust gas temperature (EGT) gauge with the probe tapped into the manifold before the turbo.
- **Boost:** manual boost controller; boost relief valve disabled. The injection pump is still at factory settings.
- **Oil cooling:** an uprated 19-row air-to-oil cooler with sandwich plate, in addition to VW's own oil-to-coolant cooler.
- **Planned:** a larger front-mounted intercooler (Alloyworks core, 51/60 mm Superflex hoses), following Karl Barrs' method in [Tuning the turbo-charged LT](https://vwlt.co.uk/tuning-the-turbo-charged-lt-a-not-so-short-guide/). Boost stays at or below 1.0 bar until it is fitted.

## Habitation modifications

The camper side has been rebuilt around an off-grid electrical system. All of this is owner work.

- **Rooflights (Oct–Nov 2020):** three Dometic/Seitz rooflights cut in by hand — a Heki 2 (960 × 655 mm, with blind and flynet), a Micro Heki 280 and a Mini S Heki (400 × 400 mm). [Video](https://youtube.com/shorts/9dqNelYHAo0).
- **Fridge (Sep 2021):** Vitrifrigo C50i 12 V compressor fridge in place of the original absorption unit.
- **Battery:** four 280 Ah LiFePO4 cells (12 V, about 3.5 kWh), top-balanced, in a removable ply box in the 40 × 40 cm space under the rear seat.
- **Battery management:** REC Active BMS with cell balancing, main contactor and precharge unit; it talks to the Victron kit over CAN so it can stop charging or cut loads on a fault.
- **Charging:** 240 W roof solar (two Phaesun Sun Plus 120 panels in series) into a Victron SmartSolar MPPT 100/30; Victron Orion XS 50 A DC-DC charger from the alternator; shore power through a Victron MultiPlus 12/800/35 inverter-charger. A 110 W flexible panel is carried as a spare.
- **Monitoring and control:** Victron Cerbo GX on a 4G router, so the system can be checked and the fridge and heater switched remotely. Fresh-water tank fitted with a float sender that reads out in litres.
- **230 V:** RCBO-protected kitchen socket fed from the inverter.
- **Heating:** Eberspächer Airtronic diesel heater (2013).
- **Build log:** [Shed7 — Van Power](https://reports.workphotos.com/6987670f2f0b100fc1753817).

## Maintenance highlights

The van has a paper trail back to 1999, and receipts since 2011 logged against the odometer.

**Engine rebuild, May 1999 (Germany).** Autohaus Kath in Rendsburg removed and rebuilt the engine with a new short block, cylinder head, water pump, radiator, clutch disc and cam belt — 12,883 DM including diagnostics at VW Bremen and a heater repair that summer.

**Cam belt and water pump, Nov 2021.** Belt, tensioner, idler and a Pierburg water pump, parts bought from Brickwerks and fitted by John Clark VW at 129,825 miles.

**Steering and front end, 2025–26.** Worn steering idlers rebuilt with two repair kits sourced from Germany, nearside track rod and end replaced, front ball joints and wheel alignment done. Glow plugs replaced on cylinders 1–5 (number 6 needs the injection pump off). Clutch kit and belts replaced Apr 2026.

**Diesel tank, Jul 2026.** The original steel tank had rusted through and was leaking onto the waste-water tank below it. Both tanks came off; the tank was replaced with the last new one Brickwerks had, painted in epoxy, and refitted with a new float valve, seals and fuel lines. The chassis above was wire-brushed, treated and waxed. [Shed7 — Diesel tank](https://reports.workphotos.com/6a929e358c89c1398f170f59).

**Cooling system, Aug–Sep 2026.** After overheating on a run home from Talla, the fan was found with a blade missing: the perished rubber cowl seal had come off its seat and been drawn into the fan. Radiator off and flushed; new fan wheel on the original 1993 viscous coupling; new 80 °C thermostat; perished alternator cooling hose replaced in silicone; refilled with G12 EVO coolant. [Shed7 — Van Cooling](https://reports.workphotos.com/6aa7cf5961b8e3490f80c44e).

**Corrosion, Jul 2026 onwards.** Underside being treated in stages with the Bilt Hamber system: wash, mechanical prep, Deox-Gel, Hydrate-80, Epoxy-Mastic, then Dynax S50 and Dynax UC wax.

**Bodywork.** Resprayed in 2015 and again (with bodywork) in 2020; roof gel-coat restored; new side-stripe decals by Kenwil.

## Where it stands now

Jobs open as of October 2026:

- **MOT:** front tyres rub on the anti-roll bar links; clearance fix being worked out before the retest.
- **Towbar:** factory towbar, rear step and 13-pin electrics fitted; the two bolts above the towball still to go in, then a torque check after some miles.
- **Cooling:** new thermostat still to be proven on a long climb; the rubber cowl seal (VW 076 121 289) is unobtainable new, so a repair or used part is being sought.
- **Gas:** the underslung LPG tank is original and unserviced; it is due an inspection with the tank dropped, and likely replacement alongside fitting a Truma Ultrastore water heater.
- **Due:** brake fluid; tyres are from 2020 and nearing replacement age.
- **Planned:** larger intercooler; underside corrosion work continues.
