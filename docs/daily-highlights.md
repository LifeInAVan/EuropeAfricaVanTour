Each place links to its source: the Wikipedia article (description and location) or, where there is no article, its OpenStreetMap location. The route is drawn through these coordinates.

Not daily: Morocco winter base (3 Jan–7 Feb 2028, excursions roughly weekly, rest days at the coast; Toubkal 9–12 Feb) and home time in Pärnu (25 Jun–11 Jul 2028, Laulupidu 30 Jun–9 Jul).
