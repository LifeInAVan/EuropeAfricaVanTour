# Route change list 2: slower pace and base camps

Prepared 27 Oct 2026 against the route as it was on 25 Oct (departure 19 Jul, year 2027 behind the scenes). **This list replaces the first change list of 26 Oct.** Everything from that list that still applies is repeated here, so apply this one only. Anything not listed stays as it is.

Distances are OSRM car figures; the LT31 will be about 30% slower. Two files come with this list:
- `bases.json`: the bases, nights, campsites and day trips (format below).
- `reference-dates.json`: the date of every place in my working copy, to check the date pins against.

## What changed and why

Peter's rule: at least two days a week without driving, and **about 70 miles (113 km) per driving day on average**. To meet it:
- Morocco becomes one loop, and the van is stored at Málaga over the winter while we fly home.
- The homecoming uses the Kiel–Klaipėda ferry, and the Polish coast, Estonian sights and Riga are dropped.
- The Arctic goes via Kilpisjärvi instead of Nordkapp. The fjords are kept, and the trip finishes on 26 Sep instead of 22 Aug.
- Places costing a long detour are dropped (list by chapter below).
- We travel base to base. The van stays 1–5 nights at each base, and nearby places are seen on foot, by bike or by train.

**Result:**
- About **16,700 van miles** (26,900 km), against about 24,000 miles before.
- 238 driving days at about 70 miles each, and 2.1 days a week without driving.
- 107 bases and 286 places (377 before).

Two exceptions are intended: the first week (Pärnu–Kraków) and the run from home to Tromsø for the midnight sun.

## Base camps on the map (new)

**Data:** add `data/bases.json` (supplied). Each base has:
- `id`, `name` (town), `center_place` (the place it sits at), `lat`, `lon`
- `arrive`, `leave`, `nights`, `chapter`
- `station` (railway station within 3 km, or null)
- `campsites`: up to two, with name, source link and distance; empty means "to find"
- `ferry_in`: the ferry taken to reach it, or null
- `drive_in_km` and `drive_days`: the drive from the previous base, and the days needed at about 113 km a day
- `places`: day trips from the base, each with `title`, `how` (walk, bike or train), straight-line `km` and `date`
- `on_the_way`: places seen during the drive in, each with `title` and `date`

Day-trip rules used: walk up to 2 km, bike up to 25 km, train up to 60 km where both the base and the place have a station within 3 km. No buses: most regional buses refuse dogs over 10 kg.

**Map:**
- A solid line for the van, base to base (road geometry through any on-the-way places), and dashed lines for ferries.
- Bases as filled circles sized by nights; on-the-way places as small hollow markers on the line.
- Day trips as thin dashed lines from the base to each place, coloured by how: green for bike, blue for train, grey for walking.
- Base popup: town, dates, nights, miles to get there, ferry, station, day trips, campsite links.

**Timeline:** each base as a bar for its nights, so the days without driving show.

**Film:** pause on each base and draw its day trips outward before the van moves on.

A working preview of this layout is in Peter's artifacts as "Base Camp Map".

## Depends on the start year

- **Laulupidu:** 30 Jun – 9 Jul 2028 anchors part two. It is held every five years.
- **La Vuelta:** dates and stages change every year. 2027 is 4–26 Sep, starting in Santiago.
- **Easter-based events:** Cádiz Carnival, Jerez, La Magdalena, Colmar markets and the Easter fountains. Easter 2028 is 16 Apr.
- **Ramadan:** about 8 Feb – 9 Mar 2027 and 28 Jan – 27 Feb 2028. With Morocco now in November–December 2027 it does not affect this trip. A later start year may bring it into the Morocco dates.
- **Toubkal in late November:** early snow is possible, and a guide is compulsory.
- **Events held only in some years:**
  - Villaviciosa cider festival: odd years
  - Santiago Holy Year: 2027
  - Évora European Capital of Culture: 2027
  - Gjirokastër folk festival: expected 2028
- **Weekday-based items:**
  - markets: Rissani Thursday, Asni Saturday, Barcelos Thursday, Feira da Ladra Tuesday and Saturday
  - the Kanal and Boka Night events
- **Ferry seasons:** Gryllefjord–Andenes is summer only. The Kiel–Klaipėda timetable needs checking for May 2028.
- **Norwegian campsites:** many close in mid-September. Check the Bergen and Oslo bases (12–20 Sep).
- **Midnight sun:** set by latitude, so the dates are the same every year (Tromsø to about 25 Jul, Senja to about 24 Jul).
